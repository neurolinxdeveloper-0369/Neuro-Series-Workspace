Request account deletion

You can request deletion of your Neuro Touch account and associated personal data through Neurolinx support. You do not need to install the app or sign in to this page.

Request deletion on WhatsApp

Support: +91 79955 58994. You may also send the request directly to this number in WhatsApp.

Opening the link prepares a message. Send the message to submit your request. This is a support-assisted process; opening this page or uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

How to request deletion

  1. Send: “Please delete my Neuro Touch account and associated personal data.”
  2. Include the email address or phone number you use to sign in. If possible, contact us from the WhatsApp number registered to your account.
  3. Support will verify that you own the account, explain any shared-home impact or necessary retention, and confirm when the deletion has been completed. If you no longer have access to your registered contact, explain this to support so ownership can be reviewed.

Never send your Google password, Wi-Fi password or sign-in verification codes to support. Do not request deletion of an account you do not own. Contacting support through WhatsApp is subject to WhatsApp's privacy practices.

Data covered by the request

  • Your account profile, account identifiers and linked sign-in records.
  • Your household memberships, invitations and account-linked preferences and notifications.
  • Personal home and room information, saved Wi-Fi credentials, device associations, routines and associated activity records belonging exclusively to the deleted account.

If you own a home shared with other people, tell support whether ownership should be transferred before account deletion. Deleting your membership does not delete another person's account or the home and device records that they continue to own.

Retention and device limitations

Deletion applies to the account and associated personal data in the active service after ownership has been verified. Information may need to be retained where necessary for a legal obligation or a documented security purpose. Support will identify any such data, the reason and applicable retention period when handling your request. This page does not promise automatic deletion within 90 days or an immediate purge of backup copies.

Account deletion does not factory-reset physical hardware, erase Wi-Fi credentials stored on that hardware, or delete data independently held by Google or WhatsApp. Follow your device's reset instructions before transferring it to another person. Deleting Neuro Touch access also does not delete your Google or WhatsApp account.
