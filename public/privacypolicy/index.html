NEURO TOUCH · NEUROLINX

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Neurolinx collects and uses information when you use Neuro Touch, including the Android app, iOS app and web service at neurolinx.in/neuro-series. Neuro Touch helps you set up and manage connected devices, homes, rooms and routines.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: your email address or phone number, account identifier, and profile information provided through your chosen sign-in method. Google sign-in supplies account identifiers and available profile details. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Home and room information: names, property details, room assignments, sharing permissions, and any address, coordinates or time zone you provide.
  • Wi-Fi setup information: the home network name (SSID) and password you choose to save, and nearby network information used during supported mobile setup. Saved home Wi-Fi passwords are encrypted at rest and are supplied to a device when you provision it.
  • Connected-device information: device identifiers and models, network and connection information, online status, switch states, commands and acknowledgements, readings such as energy usage where supported, alerts, activity and routine history.
  • Service and support information: verification requests, technical logs, IP addresses and errors needed to operate and troubleshoot the service, along with information you send to support.
  • Assistant requests: messages you submit to the assistant and relevant information about the home and devices you can access, used to answer questions or process requested actions.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, connect and control devices, save your home configuration, execute routines, show readings and history, manage household access, deliver verification codes and alerts, answer support requests, and maintain service reliability and security.

3. Permissions and information stored on your phone

  • Nearby Wi-Fi and location: supported mobile platforms may require nearby-device or location permission to discover and connect to Wi-Fi during setup. GPS coordinates are used when you choose location detection. Availability depends on the platform.
  • Camera: used when you choose to scan a device or invitation QR code.
  • Microphone: used if you choose a supported voice-input feature. Your platform's speech-recognition service may process speech under its own terms.
  • Notifications: used for supported device and service alerts when enabled.
  • Biometrics: optional app locking delegates verification to the operating system. Neuro Touch does not receive or store your fingerprint or face template.
  • Local storage: the app or browser stores sign-in session information, selected home and preferences so the service can remember your session.

You can manage permissions in your device or browser settings. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working. Signing out removes the app's stored sign-in session; it does not delete your server account.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information is processed by the infrastructure and services needed to run Neuro Touch, including hosting and database services, Google when you choose Google sign-in, email delivery providers, and the OpenWA gateway and WhatsApp services when you request a WhatsApp code or contact support. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Assistant requests may be processed by the configured model service.

Members you invite can access home and device information according to their permissions. Wi-Fi credentials and device configuration are sent to the hardware you provision. We may disclose information where required by applicable law or to protect users and the service.

5. Retention and security

Account and home information is retained while needed to provide your service. Operational records and backups may remain for security, troubleshooting, recovery or applicable legal obligations. Verification codes expire after their configured validity period. We use measures including encrypted transport for the public service, access controls and encryption of saved home Wi-Fi passwords. Local hardware setup may use the device's setup network; keep setup credentials private. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and deletion requests

You can update supported home and device settings, manage household sharing, revoke permissions and sign out. To request access, correction or deletion of your account and associated personal data, contact Neurolinx using the support link below and state your request. We may need to verify account ownership before acting. Do not send passwords, verification codes or Wi-Fi credentials. We will explain any information that must be retained for applicable legal or security reasons.

7. Children

Neuro Touch is intended for people authorized to manage their household or connected equipment. It is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission, contact us to request review and removal.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the service or its data handling changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, contact Neurolinx / Neuro Touch support on WhatsApp: +91 79955 58994. If WhatsApp is unavailable, open Help & Support in the app to view the current support contact.
